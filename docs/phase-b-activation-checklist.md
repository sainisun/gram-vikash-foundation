# Phase B Community Activation Checklist

**Status:** Preparation only. Completing this checklist does not activate community posting, chat, or moderation routes.

> Phase B may begin only after Module 1 is stable in production and the foundation can operate the ledger. A named moderation owner and Grievance Officer decision are mandatory before user-generated content is enabled. [1]

| Requirement | Evidence required | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Module 1 operating evidence | Staging/production ledger workflow, audit review, and support owner are recorded | Founder / finance owner | Pending |
| Community guidelines | Approved public rules covering prohibited content, reporting, and member expectations | Founder / moderation owner | Pending |
| Grievance process | Named Grievance Officer, contact channel, acknowledgement target, and escalation route | Founder | Pending |
| Safeguarding escalation | Child-safety and vulnerable-person escalation contacts, response steps, and documentation boundary | Safeguarding owner | Pending |
| Moderator staffing | Named primary and backup moderators, coverage window, and access roles | Moderation owner | Pending |
| Privacy and retention | Approved privacy notice, report-retention rule, and deletion/appeal handling | Privacy owner | Pending |
| Upload safety | Storage quarantine, file validation, malware-scan adapter, and private-before-review behavior | Technical owner | Pending |
| Pilot measurement | Rate-limit, report, removal, response-time, and outage metrics defined | Technical and moderation owners | Pending |

## Technical boundary

The existing community, voter-document review, and voting routes remain visible only as guarded placeholders. They must continue to return a closed or approval-required state until the evidence above is recorded and the relevant feature flags are deliberately enabled. No community content, private messaging, government-ID document, or individual vote data should be created for testing outside the approved pilot process. [1] [2]
