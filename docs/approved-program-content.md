## Free Coaching

Gram Vikash Foundation provides free coaching support for children from financially disadvantaged families. Approved program updates will be published with aggregate progress only; no identifying child information is displayed.

## Community Library

The community library provides a shared learning space and free access to reading resources for village residents. Public updates will describe approved activities and aggregate progress without publishing private visitor information.

## Kanyadan Assistance

Kanyadan Assistance provides financial support for eligible poor families seeking wedding assistance for their daughters. The foundation handles this work with privacy by default; public information uses aggregate updates and never identifies a family without explicit written consent.

## Publication rules

Program metrics remain blank until a responsible owner approves a source-backed aggregate update. Any image requires the applicable consent and safety review. Kanyadan information must remain anonymized unless explicit written consent permits otherwise.
